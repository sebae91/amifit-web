Common questions

Why isn't my data showing up after reinstalling the app?

AmiFit stores your data locally on your device. If you deleted the app, your local data was deleted with it. If you had iCloud Backup enabled on your device before deletion, your data may have been backed up and can be restored by reinstalling the app on the same Apple ID with iCloud Backup active.

Can I use AmiFit on multiple devices?

AmiFit is designed for a single device. It does not sync between devices in real time. If you restore from an iCloud backup, your data will transfer to the new device, but changes on one device won't automatically appear on another.

How do I cancel my monthly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by AmiFit. To cancel, go to your iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, find AmiFit, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of your current billing period.

I bought lifetime access. What does that include?

Lifetime access gives you permanent access to all premium nutrient tracking — vitamins, minerals, omega-3 fatty acids, amino acids, and all 50+ tracked nutrients — for as long as you have the app installed. No future charges. No expiry.

A food I eat isn't in the database. Can I add it?

Yes. AmiFit lets you create custom foods with full nutritional details. You can also share custom foods with other devices using the built-in QR code export — no internet needed. If you'd like to suggest a food for a future database update, email us.

Does AmiFit work on older iPhones?

AmiFit requires iOS 18.0 or later. It runs on any iPhone that supports iOS 18, including older models like iPhone XR and iPhone SE (3rd generation).
