Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 12, 2026

The short version: AmiFit does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

AmiFit is an iOS application for tracking nutritional intake. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. What Data AmiFit Collects

AmiFit collects no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, health data, or any other identifying information.

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Foods and meals you log in the diary
  • Personal settings (daily calorie goal, activity level, personal targets)
  • Custom foods or recipes you create

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up via iCloud (see section 4).

3. No Analytics, No Tracking, No Third-Party SDKs

AmiFit contains no analytics libraries, no crash reporting tools, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind. There is no code in the app that communicates with external servers.

4. iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device (a setting controlled by you in your iPhone Settings), your AmiFit data may be included in your personal iCloud backup. This backup is controlled entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy. AmiFit itself does not initiate or manage iCloud sync.

AmiFit does not have access to your iCloud data, and no AmiFit data is sent to any server other than Apple's iCloud infrastructure (if you have backup enabled).

5. No Account Required

AmiFit does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any identifying information. The app works without any registration.

6. Children's Privacy

AmiFit does not knowingly collect any information from anyone. Since no data is collected at all, there are no special concerns related to children's privacy.

7. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, its security is governed by your device's security settings (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We recommend keeping your device secured with a passcode or biometric authentication.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published at www.amifit.app/privacy with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
support@amifit.app
