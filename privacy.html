Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 16, 2026

Offline. No account ever created.

AmiFit is a local app: all your data lives on your device. There are no accounts to create, no servers storing your information, and no way for us to see what you do. Your nutrition data, health information, goals, and everything else you enter stays on your device and nowhere else.

Premium Purchases

If you purchase AmiFit Premium, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information. A monthly subscription requires a periodic internet connection for Apple to verify your subscription status. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase, and once completed, the app never needs an internet connection again.

Deleting Your Data

All your data is stored on your device. To delete everything, simply delete the app. There is nothing on our end to remove.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, reach out to us at info@amifit.app.
